FireSat-S — Satellite-Based Wildfire Early Warning System

Embedded Systems Engineer

Overview
FireSat-S is a CubeSat concept designed for early wildfire detection in Algeria and Tunisia. The concept combines MWIR thermal imaging, TinyML, and smart power management to detect potential wildfire events while reducing onboard power consumption.
My contribution
I worked on the onboard embedded system and power management concept, contributing to the MOSFET-based power-gating system and FreeRTOS architecture. I also contributed to the integration concept between the imaging system and TinyML processing, with a focus on making the onboard system more power-efficient.
